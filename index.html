A quiet table in the old city

King’s Joy sits on Wudaoying Hutong in the Guozijian area of Dongcheng. Dishes arrive on stone, porcelain and drifting mist, and a harp plays while you eat.

Come for a long lunch, a slow dinner or a table outside when the weather is kind.

  • Outdoor seatingTables outside for fine weather.
  • Vegan optionsPlant-based dishes on the menu.
  • Live musicMusic in the room while you dine.
